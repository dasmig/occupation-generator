Employment status, education, the share of employment by ISCO-08 group, and
the age and education factors (resources/*/status.tsv, education.tsv,
shares.tsv and factors.tsv) are derived from the International Labour
Organization's labour force statistics, ILOSTAT (https://ilostat.ilo.org),
indicators EMP_TEMP_SEX_OC2_NB_A, EMP_TEMP_SEX_AGE_OCU_NB_A,
EMP_TEMP_SEX_OCU_EDU_NB_A, POP_XWAP_SEX_AGE_EDU_NB_A, POP_XWAP_SEX_AGE_NB_A,
EMP_TEMP_SEX_AGE_NB_A and UNE_TUNE_SEX_AGE_NB_A, downloaded on 2026-10-14.
Source: ILOSTAT, ILO. ILOSTAT data is published under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0); the values here are
percentages and factors computed from the ILO's counts, not the ILO's own
figures, and the ILO has not endorsed them.

The titles of the 43 ISCO-08 level-2 groups (resources/*/isco.tsv) follow the
International Standard Classification of Occupations 2008 (ILO).

The job catalogue (occupations.tsv: jobs, weights, age limits, tags, skills,
gear), the split of the inactive by age (inactivity.tsv), the education age
limits, and the mapping of jobs to ISCO-08 groups were written for this
library and are released with it under the MIT License (see LICENSE.MIT).
O*NET and ESCO were used as a guide to what skills a job has; no text from
either was copied.
